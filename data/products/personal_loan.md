# Personal Loan

## Overview
A Personal Loan is an unsecured loan — meaning no collateral or asset needs to be pledged — that a customer can take for any personal financial need such as a medical emergency, home renovation, education expenses, or a wedding. The bank lends a fixed amount upfront, which the customer repays in fixed monthly installments (EMIs) over a chosen tenure. Because no security is required, personal loans are quicker to process than secured loans but carry higher interest rates.

## Eligibility & documents required
- Any salaried or self-employed individual aged 21 to 60 years with a stable income can apply.
- Minimum monthly income: ₹15,000.
- A good credit score (CIBIL score of 700 or above) improves approval chances and may result in a lower interest rate.
- Documents required: PAN card, Aadhaar card, last 3 months salary slips or income proof (ITR for self-employed), last 6 months bank statement, one passport-size photo, and a filled loan application form.
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹15,00,000 depending on income and credit profile.

## Interest rates / returns
- Interest rate: 10.5% to 15% per annum, depending on the applicant's credit score, income, and employment type.
- Tenure: 1 year to 5 years, chosen at the time of application.
- EMI is calculated on a reducing balance basis — meaning interest is charged only on the outstanding principal, not the full original amount.
- A higher credit score typically qualifies the customer for a lower interest rate and better terms.

## Risks / things to know
- Missing an EMI attracts a late payment penalty and, more importantly, negatively impacts your CIBIL credit score — making future loans harder to obtain.
- Prepayment is allowed but may attract a prepayment charge of 2% to 3% on the outstanding amount depending on tenure remaining.
- Personal loans have higher interest rates than secured loans (home loans, gold loans) because no collateral is involved — compare carefully before applying.
- A common mistake is borrowing more than one can repay comfortably. A general rule: total EMI obligations should not exceed 40% of monthly income.
- Interest paid on a personal loan is not tax-deductible (unlike home loan interest), so factor this into the true cost of borrowing.

## How to apply
- Online: Log in to net banking or the mobile app, go to "Loans," select "Personal Loan," fill in the application, upload required documents, and submit. Approval and disbursal typically within 24 to 48 hours for eligible applicants.
- Offline: Visit the nearest branch with all required documents. Branch staff can help assess eligibility and suggest an appropriate loan amount and tenure based on income.
- Nearest branch details can be provided by the assistant if requested.
